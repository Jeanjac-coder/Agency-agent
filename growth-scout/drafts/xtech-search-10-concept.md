## 1. Title

Vybe: screenless overnight readiness signals against each Soldier's own baseline

## 2. Problem

- Readiness decisions lean on self-report and on one-night scores compared with
  population averages, and those miss a Soldier's personal drift.
- Screens and alerts on wrist devices can be a light, distraction or OPSEC concern in the
  field and in barracks.
- Unit H2F teams need data they can pull into their own tools, not another closed app.

## 3. Solution

- A screenless band worn overnight that reads heart rate, HRV and sleep timing.
- Each night is compared with the wearer's own rolling baseline, so the signal is a change
  from that Soldier's normal, not a population score.
- A developer SDK lets unit data teams and H2F staff pull nightly values into the tools
  they already use.

## 4. Army relevance

- Fits H2F's sleep readiness and physical readiness domains.
- Supports leaders spotting sustained drift from baseline after field exercises, shift
  work or travel, as a prompt for a conversation, not a diagnosis.
- An SDK-first design avoids lock-in and fits existing Army data workflows.
